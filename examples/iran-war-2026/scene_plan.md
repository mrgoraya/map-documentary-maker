# Scene plan: The 2026 Iran War, Explained (16:9, 1080p, 30 fps)

Timing comes from the voiceover v2 (Whisper word timings, `work/doc1/sentences.json`). Times are **local to each part**, because each part is built and rendered separately. Parts 2–4 each open with a silent 3 s chapter card before 0.0, which brings the total from 5:44 to about 5:53.

**Types:** Map / Object anim / Clipping / Diagram / Photo / Stock / Title. All clippings are recreated text cards (exact quote + outlet + date), not screenshots. The `#` numbers in the Asset column point to the asset checklist at the bottom of this file.

## Global look
- **Map:** dark, desaturated relief basemap from Natural Earth, already in the project. The style guide's accent colours:
  - Iran: teal.
  - Israel and the US side: orange.
  - Strike and hit marks: red.
  - Labels: Inter or Bebas, all caps, white with a soft shadow.
  - Slow camera drift on every shot.
- **Kit reused:** ships, drones, jets, targets and lettering from the news Shorts (`src/news2`, `src/news5`), restyled for the dark map.
- **Sound:** synthesised SFX under the voice (whooshes, jets, impacts, typewriter ticks on date cards). **No music.**
- **Labels:** every number on screen carries a small source label ("HRANA", "CENTCOM says", "Saudi authorities", …).

## Asset checklist
Photos of real people come only from free-licence sources, never generated. Every photo gets a small credit line on screen and in the description.

1. **[REQUIRED] Photo of Ali Khamenei** (Scene 3)
   - Source: Wikimedia Commons, khamenei.ir official photo, CC BY 4.0.
   - Fallback: a name card on the map.
2. **[OPTIONAL] Donald Trump official portrait** (Scenes 10, 41, 60)
   - Source: White House or Commons, public domain.
   - Fallback: text-only quote cards.
3. **[OPTIONAL] J.D. Vance official portrait** (Scene 34)
   - Source: Commons, public domain.
   - Fallback: Islamabad map with a text card.
4. **[OPTIONAL] Photo of Mojtaba Khamenei** (Scene 31)
   - Use only if Commons has a free-licence image.
   - Fallback: a text name card.
5. **[OPTIONAL] Photo of Badr Albusaidi, Oman FM** (Scene 13)
   - Source: Commons.
   - Fallback: the clipping card alone.
6. **[OPTIONAL] Photos of USS Abraham Lincoln and Gerald R. Ford** (Scene 11)
   - Source: navy.mil / DVIDS, public domain.
   - Fallback: carrier icons on the map.
7. **[OPTIONAL] Stock video: LNG carrier, and a tanker at sea at night** (Scenes 39, 50a)
   - Source: Pexels (free licence), labelled "illustrative".
   - Fallback: ship icons on the map.
8. **Article screenshots: not needed.** Every "Source:" clipping is a recreated card with the exact checked quote, outlet and date, so there are no copyright problems.
9. **Not used:** the Minab school (map and text only), protest photos (dots on the map), and generated images of real people.
10. **Already have:** the Natural Earth map data, the news-Short kit (`src/news2`, `src/news5`), the Think with MG logo, and the SFX scripts.

**File naming:** `public/doc1/scene03_khamenei.jpg`, `scene11_carrier_lincoln.jpg`, and so on, with a `credits.txt` beside them (author, licence, URL).
