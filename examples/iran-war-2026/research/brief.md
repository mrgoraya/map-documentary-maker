# Research Brief: The 2026 Iran war (Iran, Israel, US)

Think with MG documentary 1. Compiled Nov 25, 2026. Most facts here have two independent sources. Anything with only one is marked *single-source*. Claims are credited to whoever made them. The November items marked "verified" come from the Think with MG news Shorts 1, 2, 5 and 6, which were fact-checked earlier.

## Summary
After the 12-day war of June 2025, the nuclear standoff got worse:
- UN sanctions came back in September 2025.
- Iran's economy and a January 2026 protest crackdown deepened the crisis.
- The US built up its forces while Oman-mediated talks went on.

On Sat Feb 28, 2026 the US ("Operation Epic Fury") and Israel ("Roaring Lion") struck Iran and killed Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei. Iran fired back at Israel and at six Arab states that host US forces, and closed the Strait of Hormuz. What followed:
1. A Pakistan-brokered ceasefire on Apr 7.
2. A US naval blockade from Apr 13.
3. A 14-point memorandum on Jun 17. It collapsed on Jul 7–8.
4. Since mid-July, a blockade-versus-Hormuz standoff with on-and-off strikes.

As of Nov 25 there is no truce, and new US-Israeli strikes are reported to be under consideration.

## Toll (use "at least"; every figure is incomplete)
- **Iran:** Legal Medicine Organization 3,375 dead as of 12 Apr; Foundation of Martyrs 3,468 (19 Apr) [83]. HRANA counted at least 3,636 to 8 Apr. No official update after April.
- **Lebanon:** more than 4,300 killed since 2 Mar [84][82].
- **US military:** 23 dead, 865 wounded (Pentagon, late Oct) [85][86].
- **Israel:** no reliable current total found. Leave it out of the script.
- **Seafarers:** 25 killed around Hormuz since February (UN) [92].
- **Displacement:** up to 3.2 million displaced inside Iran (UNHCR, March) [87][88].
- **Oil:** Brent ~$70 before the war, above $100 on 12 Mar, below $90 on 24 Nov [34][82]. IEA: no "credible pathway" back to pre-war Hormuz [89] *(quote, single outlet)*.

## Conflicting figures
- **Minab school deaths:** 108 (Iranian judiciary), 115+ (governor), ~165, 170+ (US senators). Who carried it out is disputed. Recommended wording: "Iranian officials say at least a hundred and sixty-five died."
- **Riyadh airport deaths:** 12 (Saudi authorities), 13 or 14 (others). Recommended wording: "Saudi authorities said twelve."
- **Iran's war dead:** 3,375 to 7,650 depending on source. Recommended wording: "Iran's own count passed three thousand by April."
- **Dates that shift by time zone:** ceasefire 7 or 8 Apr; memorandum 17 or 18 Jun; Mojtaba 8 or 9 Mar; Jordan attack 17 or 18 Jul. The script uses the US date.
