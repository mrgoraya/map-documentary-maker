**Fact-check log**
- First draft said "Only Saudi Arabia and the UAE have pipelines that can go around it." The chokepoints page says pipelines in Saudi Arabia, the UAE **and Iran** provide alternatives, so "only" was wrong. Narration changed to "Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates have pipelines that can bypass the strait. But they can carry only a fraction of that oil." (2.6-4.7 of about 20 million b/d.)
- Left out: the strait's width (sources measure different things) and "the world's most important chokepoint" (EIA says "one of the world's most important oil chokepoints", and calls Malacca the largest by volume).
